# Week 1-2 Core Patterns (Arrays, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack)

## Target rhythm (10-15 hours/week)
- 4 focused practice days
- 1 review day
- 1 mock day
- 1 light day (flashcards + complexity recall)

## Day-by-day plan

### Week 1
- Day 1: Arrays/Hashing fundamentals (2 easy, 1 medium)
- Day 2: Arrays/Hashing (1 easy, 2 medium)
- Day 3: Two Pointers basics (2 easy, 1 medium)
- Day 4: Two Pointers progression (1 easy, 2 medium)
- Day 5: Review all misses (no notes, timed)
- Day 6: Mock block (2 mediums, 35 minutes each, explain out loud)
- Day 7: Light day (flashcards + update mistake log)

### Week 2
- Day 8: Sliding Window fixed-size + variable-size
- Day 9: Sliding Window progression (focus on invariants)
- Day 10: Stack fundamentals (parentheses, monotonic ideas)
- Day 11: Stack progression (1 easy, 2 medium)
- Day 12: Review all misses (no notes, timed)
- Day 13: Mock block (2 mediums, 35 minutes each, explain out loud)
- Day 14: Light day (flashcards + KPI review)
