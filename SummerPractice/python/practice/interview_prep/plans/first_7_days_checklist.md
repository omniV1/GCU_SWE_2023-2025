# First 7 Days Checklist (Zero Guesswork)

Use a 90-120 minute block each day.

## Day 1 (Arrays/Hashing reset)
- Warm-up (15 min): `01_richest_customer.py`, explain O(m*n).
- Timed set (35 min each):
  - LC 1 Two Sum
  - LC 217 Contains Duplicate
- Debrief (20 min): log misses in `logs/mistake_log_template.csv`.
- Re-solve (15 min): whichever problem felt slower.

## Day 2 (Arrays/Hashing progression)
- Timed set (35 min each):
  - LC 242 Valid Anagram
  - LC 49 Group Anagrams
- Stretch (optional 35 min): LC 347 Top K Frequent Elements.
- Debrief: capture invariant and edge cases for each problem.

## Day 3 (Two Pointers basics)
- Warm-up (15 min): `03_valid_palindrome.py`.
- Timed set (35 min each):
  - LC 125 Valid Palindrome
  - LC 167 Two Sum II
- Re-solve (15 min): one previous miss from Day 1-2.

## Day 4 (Two Pointers progression)
- Timed set (35 min each):
  - LC 283 Move Zeroes
  - LC 11 Container With Most Water
- Stretch (optional): LC 15 3Sum (allow 45 min max).
- Debrief: write one sentence for the pointer invariant.

## Day 5 (Review day, no notes)
- Re-do all misses due today (from `next_redo_date`).
- Run: `python interview_prep/logs/review_scheduler.py`
- Target: each redo <=25 min, then 5 min explanation aloud.

## Day 6 (Mock day)
- Mock format:
  - 35 min: LC 121 Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock
  - 10 min: explain tradeoffs and complexity
  - 35 min: LC 3 Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters
  - 10 min: explain tradeoffs and complexity
- Fill `mocks/mock_scorecard.md` immediately after.

## Day 7 (Light day + weekly KPI)
- Flashcards (30 min): patterns + complexity targets.
- KPI update (20 min): fill one row in `logs/kpi_tracker_template.csv`.
- Retrospective (20 min): use `plans/weekly_retrospective.md`.
- Plan next week from weakest pattern only.

## Non-negotiable rules this week
- Decide pattern in first 2 minutes.
- State brute force and target complexity before coding.
- Always test 2 edge cases before done.
- Log misses same day (no backlog).
